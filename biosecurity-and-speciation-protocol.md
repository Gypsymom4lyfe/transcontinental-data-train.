Frequency Pulse: Before entering the border pass or decontamination tunnel, localized ultrasonic transducers send low-impact acoustic vibrations through the exterior airframe of "The Tabernacle."
Mechanism: This breaks the grip of clinging insects, spiders, and seed pods without damaging the hardware, making them easy to sweep off in the next stage.
B. Stage 2: Mountain Tunnel "Cold-Shock" (Cryogenic Air Blast)
Natural/Mechanical Thermal Drop: Leveraging mountain tunnel passes or specialized border archways, the train passes through an intense blast of rapid-chilled, compressed air.
Mechanism: Insects and micro-fauna are ectothermic (their body temperature depends on the air). A rapid, short exposure to freezing-cold air instantly stuns or freezes micro-fauna on the exterior shell, rendering them inactive in seconds without using any chemicals.
C. Stage 3: High-Velocity Compressed Air-Blade (The Wind Tunnel)
High-Volume Air Sweeps: Directional air-knives (similar to aerospace wind-tunnel systems) blow across the top, sides, undercarriage, and wheel wells at high velocity.
Mechanism: The neutralized organic material is cleanly swept off into a localized containment collection basin right at the border, preventing anything from crossing state lines.
